World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026

Ukraine and Russia ended two days of US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi with no ceasefire but a deal on a large prisoner swap, which US envoy Jared Kushner called the biggest release of the war (NPR). President Trump said the war with Iran was "militarily effectively over" (AP), and Secretary of State Marco Rubio said US economic pressure had made major combat operations unnecessary (NYT); Iran said the Strait of Hormuz would "never return" to its pre-war status (Al Jazeera). In Washington, House Republicans blocked an effort to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, voting 211-204 to send it to committee, as Democrats prepared to take the House after the midterms. US inflation rose to 4% in October (BLS), reviving talk of further Federal Reserve rate rises and pushing mortgage rates sharply higher. China's trade surplus passed $1 trillion in the first ten months of the year, a record pace (NYT). At the COP31 climate summit in Turkey, UN chief Antonio Guterres warned the world was on a "path to destruction" without faster action. Israeli strikes killed at least seven people in Gaza, including a father and his 13-year-old son, as the truce frayed (BBC). Anthropic's IPO roadshow drew such demand that bankers weighed pricing above its $70-$78 range, a day after its filing valued the AI firm at about $1 trillion (FT/Reuters). British police detained a US soldier in Gloucester on suspicion of spying for Russia (BBC). Brazil's President Lula met Venezuela's interim leader Delcy Rodriguez at COP31, as state oil firm Petrobras signalled interest in returning to Venezuela (Reuters). In France, 75-year-old Jean-Luc Melenchon launched a fourth bid for the presidency (AP). Also: 12 people died in a fog pile-up in Bulgaria (BBC); Sudan's drone war knocked out power; Chile's Atacama Desert bloomed on a scale unseen in decades (NPR); French Resistance hero Solange Alexandre died aged 104 (BBC).
